Frequently asked questions

Is the free plan really free?
Yes, completely free. No credit card required. All 30+ basic PDF tools are available on the free plan forever.
How do I upgrade to Pro?
Click "Get Started with Pro", create an account, and complete the checkout. Your account is upgraded instantly.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Cancel anytime from your account settings. You keep Pro access until the end of your billing period.
Are my files safe?
All files are transferred over TLS encryption and permanently deleted from our servers immediately after processing. We never store or access your documents.
What AI tools are included in Pro?
Pro includes unlimited access to AI Summary, Notes Generator, Quiz Generator, Key Points Extractor, PDF Translator, and Ask PDF (chat with your document).
What is the Business plan for?
Business is designed for teams and companies that need multiple seats, API access, bulk processing, and dedicated support. Contact us to get started.
