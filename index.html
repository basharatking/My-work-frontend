FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is FallPDF really free?
+
Yes — completely free. All 20 tools work with no signup, no credit card and no watermarks on output files. Optional Pro and Enterprise plans are available for power users who need larger files or batch processing.
Are my files safe and private?
+
Absolutely. Files are encrypted during upload via TLS and permanently deleted right after you download. We never store, read or share your documents. Zero data retention policy.
How does the smart PDF to Excel work?
+
Our tool detects tables across all pages and groups them by structure. Same headers = one merged sheet. Different table structure = new worksheet. Multi-page tables are automatically combined — no separate tab per page.
What is the file size limit?
+
Free plan: up to 25 MB. Pro plan: up to 100 MB. Enterprise plan: unlimited. For large files on the free plan, try using the Compress PDF tool first.
Does it work on mobile?
+
Yes. FallPDF is fully responsive and optimized for mobile phones and tablets. No app download required — everything runs in your browser.
How accurate is PDF to Word conversion?
+
Excellent for text-based PDFs — headings, paragraphs and basic layout are preserved well. Scanned PDFs produce plain text. For best results, use native digital PDFs rather than scanned documents.
