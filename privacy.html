Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2025 · Effective immediately

TL;DR: We don't store your files. We don't read your documents. Files are deleted immediately after processing. We don't sell your data. No account required, no tracking cookies.

1. Introduction

FallPDF ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use FallPDF's PDF tools at fallpdf.netlify.app.

2. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information necessary to operate our service:

  • Files you upload: Temporarily processed on our servers for tool execution only
  • Usage data: Anonymous page views and tool usage counts (no personal identifiers)
  • Technical data: Browser type, device type, and country for analytics only

We do not collect your name, email address, or any personally identifiable information unless you contact us directly.

3. File Handling & Storage

This is the most important section for understanding how FallPDF works:

  • Files are uploaded directly to our processing server via encrypted TLS connection
  • Files are processed in memory and temporarily stored only for the duration of the task
  • All uploaded files and output files are permanently and automatically deleted immediately after you download your result
  • We never read, analyze, index, or share the content of your documents
  • No file content is ever used for training, analytics, or any other purpose
  • Files are not backed up or retained in any form

4. Cookies & Tracking

FallPDF uses minimal, privacy-respecting analytics:

  • No advertising cookies or third-party tracking pixels
  • No cross-site tracking or behavioral profiling
  • Basic analytics to understand which tools are popular (anonymous, aggregated only)
  • Session cookies required for tool functionality only

5. Third-Party Services

FallPDF's AI tools use third-party language model APIs. When using AI features (Summary, Ask PDF, Notes, Translate, Quiz), the text content of your document may be sent to an AI API provider for processing. This content is processed ephemerally and is not stored or used for training by FallPDF. Please review the relevant AI provider's privacy policy for their data handling practices.

6. Data Security

  • All data transmission is encrypted using TLS (Transport Layer Security)
  • Our servers use AES-256 encryption for any temporary file storage
  • We follow industry best practices for server security
  • Regular security audits are conducted

7. Your Rights

Since we don't store personal data, there is typically nothing to request deletion of. However, you have the right to:

  • Request information about any data we may hold about you
  • Request deletion of any data associated with your contact with us
  • Opt out of analytics tracking (by using browser privacy settings)

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of FallPDF after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at fallpdf.netlify.app/contact.
