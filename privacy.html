Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2025 · Effective immediately

TL;DR: We don't store your files. We don't read your documents. Files are deleted immediately after processing. We don't sell your data. No account required.

1. Introduction

PDFPilot ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use PDFPilot's PDF tools.

2. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information necessary to operate our service:

  • Files you upload: Temporarily processed on our servers for tool execution only.
  • Usage data: Anonymous page views and tool usage counts (no personal identifiers).
  • Technical data: Browser type, device type, and country for analytics only.

We do not collect your name, email address, or any personally identifiable information unless you contact us directly.

3. File Handling & Storage

  • Files are uploaded directly to our processing server via encrypted TLS connection.
  • Files are processed in memory and temporarily stored only for the duration of the task.
  • All uploaded and output files are permanently deleted immediately after you download your result.
  • We never read, analyze, index, or share the content of your documents.
  • No file content is ever used for training, analytics, or any other purpose.

4. Cookies & Tracking

  • No advertising cookies or third-party tracking pixels.
  • No cross-site tracking or behavioral profiling.
  • Basic analytics to understand which tools are popular (anonymous, aggregated only).
  • Session cookies required for tool functionality only.

5. Third-Party AI Services

PDFPilot's AI tools use third-party language model APIs. When using AI features, the text content of your document may be sent to an AI API provider for processing. This content is processed ephemerally and is not stored by PDFPilot.

6. Data Security

  • All data transmission is encrypted using TLS (Transport Layer Security).
  • Our servers use AES-256 encryption for any temporary file storage.
  • Regular security audits are conducted.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at our contact page.
