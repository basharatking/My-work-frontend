About RunDocs

PDF tools built for real people

We built RunDocs because existing tools were slow, bloated and required too many signups.

Our Mission

RunDocs exists to make professional PDF processing accessible to everyone — students, freelancers, business owners and teams — without paywalls, unnecessary signups or confusing interfaces.

Every tool is designed to do one thing extremely well. No bloat. No dark patterns. Just fast, reliable PDF processing that respects your privacy.

Privacy Policy

Your privacy is our top priority. Here is exactly how we handle your files:

  • Files are uploaded over an encrypted TLS connection (HTTPS).
  • Processing happens in temporary memory — files are never written to permanent storage.
  • Files are permanently deleted immediately after you download your result.
  • We never read, index or share the content of your documents.
  • We do not sell data to advertisers or third parties.
  • We collect only anonymous, aggregated usage statistics (no personal data).
