About CatchPDF

PDF tools built
for real people

We built CatchPDF because existing tools were slow, bloated and required too many signups. We wanted something faster and cleaner.

Our Mission

CatchPDF exists to make professional PDF processing accessible to everyone — students, freelancers, business owners and teams — without paywalls, unnecessary signups or confusing interfaces.

Every tool on CatchPDF is designed to do one thing extremely well. No bloat. No dark patterns. Just fast, reliable PDF processing that respects your privacy.

Privacy Policy

Your privacy is our top priority. Here is exactly how we handle your files:

  • Files are uploaded over an encrypted TLS connection (HTTPS).
  • Processing happens in temporary memory — files are never written to permanent storage.
  • Files are permanently deleted immediately after you download your result.
  • We never read, index or share the content of your documents.
  • We do not sell data to advertisers or third parties.
  • We collect only anonymous, aggregated usage statistics (tool usage counts, no personal data).

Security

We take file security seriously. Our infrastructure uses:

  • TLS 1.3 encryption for all file transfers
  • AES-256 encryption for protect/unlock operations
  • Isolated processing environments — files from different users never interact
  • Automatic file deletion immediately after processing is complete
